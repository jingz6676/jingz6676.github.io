Layer Normalization

What is exactly zero or one?

For a nonconstant $h$ and $\varepsilon>0$, the normalized $u$ has

  1. Amean $0$, $\operatorname{mean}(u^2)=1$
  2. Bmean $0$, and $\operatorname{mean}(u^2)<1$✓
  3. Cno fixed mean

$\mathbf1\T c=0$ by construction; and $s^2=v+\varepsilon$, not $v$.

$\operatorname{mean}(u^2)=v/(v+\varepsilon)=2/3$. A needs $\varepsilon=0$; $\gamma,\beta$ then move $z$ anyway.
