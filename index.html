Self-Improving Robots

Perception, assembly, and learning through physical interaction.

I am a Faculty Fellow at NYU’s Center for Robotics and Embodied Intelligence (CREO). My research brings together robot learning, multimodal perception, and assembly reasoning.

My long-term goal is to develop robots that can assemble, test, and improve robotic systems. My current work develops foundations for this vision through vision and touch, reasoning about incomplete 3D structures, and learning to interact with the physical world.

I am on the faculty job market in robotics and embodied AI.

Toward Self-Improving Robots

Research vision

How can robots build better robots? I aim to connect understanding parts, physical assembly, and functional testing, so that the outcomes of one iteration guide the next.

  1. 01 · Current foundations

    Understand parts

    Combine multimodal perception with reasoning about incomplete shapes and their relationships.

    Perception / Assembly reasoning
  2. 02 · Future direction

    Assemble robots

    Turn structural reasoning into physical skills for manipulating parts, assembling systems, and recovering from errors.

  3. 03 · Long-term goal

    Test and improve

    Use functional tests to improve designs, assembly strategies, and control policies across successive iterations.

The loop: understand → assemble → test → improve → build again.
