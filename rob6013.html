Nov 4M8ODEs, Dynamics, and Robot Simulationmathcold-write
How does a simulator advance the world?
  • An ODE as a velocity field: ẋ = f(x,t)
  • The manipulator equation M(q)q̈ + C q̇ + g = τ written as ẋ = f(x,u)

  • Runge-Kutta: RK2 intuition, RK4, accuracy against cost
  • A pendulum simulated end to end: Euler against RK4, step size, and energy drift

Policies

Late work

Five late days for the semester, in whole days, at most three on any one set, no form and no reason required, and none once solutions are posted.

Collaboration

Discuss the problems with anyone, then write your solution alone and list who you worked with.

Using AI

Use any tool to understand something, but what you submit has to be something you could reproduce at a blackboard, because the exams ask for the same derivations the problem sets do. You sit them with one sheet of paper, both sides, prepared by you, and a scientific calculator — no phone, laptop, tablet, watch or model.

Integrity and accessibility

The Tandon Student Code of Conduct and the University Academic Integrity policy apply, and accommodations go through the Moses Center with no need to tell me why.

If something goes wrong

Illness, an emergency, a visa problem, a week where nothing worked — email me, and it is handled without documentation unless the University requires it.
